Final Settlement / End of Service

Calculate End-of-Service Indemnity + Leave Encashment as per Kuwait Labour Law No. 6 of 2010 (Articles 51, 53 & 70).

Total Final Settlement
KD 0.000
Explanatory Notes (Kuwait Law)

1. End-of-Service Indemnity (Art 51): For monthly-paid employees — 15 days’ salary for each of the first 5 years + 1 full month’s salary for each year after 5. Maximum cap = 18 months’ salary. Daily rate = Monthly Basic ÷ 26.

2. Resignation reduction (Art 53): < 3 years = 0% · 3 to <5 years = 50% · 5 to <10 years = 2/3 · ≥10 years = 100%. Full amount is paid on termination by employer (without gross misconduct).

3. Leave Salary / Encashment (Art 70): Unused annual leave is paid as: Daily Rate × Unused Leave Days. Annual leave entitlement is 30 days per year.

4. What is included: Indemnity is calculated on Basic Salary (fixed allowances may be included if contractually fixed; overtime & variable pay are excluded).

Kuwait VAT Notes

Kuwait VAT: Standard rate historically discussed at 5% under GCC framework. Many local supplies and specific sectors may be zero-rated or exempt. Always confirm with your tax advisor / Ministry of Finance guidance for the current year.

Typical service companies (manpower, contracting): Check whether supplies are taxable, zero-rated (0%), or exempt. Export of services may differ from domestic.

In this app: Tag invoices/bills with VAT treatment for reporting. This is not a tax filing system.
